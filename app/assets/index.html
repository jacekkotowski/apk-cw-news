How to use
    
News. Feeds tab: add a feed from the catalogue. Listen tab: Play. Each headline is sent as Morse.


    The band. Band tab: Play, then turn the knob (or tap the waterfall, or use the Signal buttons) to find stations. Tap Show what they send to check what you copied.


    A contact. First set your callsign, name and QTH under Your station below. On the Band tab tap Answer when a station calls CQ, or Call CQ on a clear spot. Build your over with the buttons (RST, Name, QTH, Weather, Rig, Ant) and tap Send, or tap Send this for the suggested text. The partner is patient: it asks again, slows down and never fails you. Net puts the dial on its signal. End contact logs it.


    With a key. Set Send with to My key, then tap Set up my key on the Band tab (about 8 seconds; repeat it when the noise changes). Key the suggested text; the line You sent shows what was read.

With "My key" the phone listens to your key's buzzer through the microphone, on the Band tab only; set it up there. Typing always works too. Patient partners wait for you, ask again kindly and never fail a contact.

Band

  

  

  

  

  
The band is always busy. Pick the band weather: Summer static is the default. It follows the clock from where you are: nearby stations by day, distant ones after dark, and nearby ones drop into the skip zone at night. Evenings and weekends are busier, and on the big contest weekends the band fills with contest stations.
